# SR-001 — Three-slot rotation challenger, protocol v1.0

Locked November 3, 2026, before inspection of blocked-signal outcomes. Research only. This registration does not authorize a deployment, a paper-pipeline change, a trade, or an action on UNH. CH-003 remains unsupported; no fourth-slot or alternate threshold arm is registered here.

**Hypothesis:** A single, predefined replacement rule improves net account P&L versus the unchanged Champion without increasing dollar drawdown or breaching the same cash and risk limits.

## Design and sole rule

Run paired offline accounts from identical $500 settled cash, no positions and identical point-in-time inputs. Freeze the Champion source, configuration, candidate ordering, data contract, exchange calendar and simulator hashes in an outcome-blind run manifest before the first session. Control uses the Champion's unmodified decisions. Both arms use the same cash-only ledger: three slots, $100 settled reserve, $125 per position, $5 planned loss, $10 daily loss, $50 drawdown halt and two entries per day, plus all other frozen Champion gates. Both arms use identical exit priorities and simulation rules. Do not claim exact production parity where the shared simulator differs.

1. At a regular Champion decision before its entry cutoff, consider rotation only when all three slots are occupied and a new stock candidate is TRADEABLE and passes all entry gates except the slot limit and, conditionally, cash that will be freed by settlement. Select the first such candidate in the frozen Champion ordering; use ticker ascending only for a true tie. No ranking by future outcomes.
2. Eligible incumbents have completed at least 10 regular sessions **after** the fill session, have no ordinary exit pending, and have a fresh executable bid at or below their entry cost per share including entry costs. Select the earliest entry timestamp, then ticker ascending. No eligible incumbent means no rotation. This age-and-price rule is a fixed research assumption, not an inferred optimum and not an unconditional time stop.
3. At signal time, require a conservative cash projection: current settled cash plus modeled net incumbent sale proceeds by settlement must allow the frozen candidate sizing and the $100 reserve. Preserve all other cash obligations. Submit a simulated exit for the selected incumbent only; ordinary exits take priority. Use the first eligible subsequent quote within that session and the existing exit cutoff; if none exists, cancel and count the failure.
4. Reserve the vacancy for the selected ticker until the end of the next regular session. At each eligible cycle, revalidate the same ticker from fresh inputs and recompute size and all gates. Count only actual settled funds; do not borrow against pending proceeds. Enter at the next executable quote before cutoff only if every gate still passes. If revalidation, cash, or timing fails, leave the exit in the challenger record, log the orphan exit, and release the reservation at expiry. Do not replace it with a hand-picked ticker. Only one rotation attempt per session and one pending reservation at a time. A normal fill counts toward the daily limit.

## Evidence and sample

October 27–November 2 is diagnostic only. Preserve all 65 logged evaluations and summarize duplicates by ET session, symbol, side and signal episode; keep separate raw and deduplicated counts. Join canonical shadow IDs only through an explicit stored mapping or a unique match on symbol, side, cycle timestamp and configuration. Flag unmatched/ambiguous rows; never infer a per-signal result from aggregate counts. Keep October 26 daily-cap cases separate. Audit cutoff, freshness, cash and position state as available. Do not open blocked-signal or challenger outcome columns for this diagnosis.

The main window is the first 60 consecutive regular exchange sessions after November 3 **starting only once** the outcome-blind manifest and independent collector are ready before the first open. No backfilling, optional extension, or early stopping based on returns. Archive source and receipt timestamps, candidate order and eligibility, quotes, position state, and all exclusions for both arms. Inputs must have been available by the decision; an incomplete or late bar cannot be substituted. Any change to frozen rules invalidates this version. Keep missing sessions in the denominator; missing decision-critical data that could change either account path makes the efficacy result inconclusive.

## Fills, scoring and decision

Use executable ask for buys and bid for sells on the first eligible subsequent quote, with fees and adverse slippage of 15 bps per side; repeat the identical policy at 30 bps per side as the registered stress case. No midpoint, same-signal-bar or assumed gap-through-stop price. Use the same frozen conservative ordinary-stop and target policy in both arms. Apply the project T+1 settlement model on the frozen calendar; preflight quote provenance, fee schedule, settlement and fill logic on synthetic fixtures before outcome access. If the required inputs cannot be supplied, report inconclusive rather than substitute a different model.

Primary score is challenger minus control terminal net account P&L at 15 bps. Mark both arms at the final common executable closing bid less modeled exit costs, including outstanding cash receivables once; no fictitious settled buying power. Compute dollar drawdown from daily net liquidation equity including the $500 starting point, and report intraday risk halts separately. Report turnover, daily paired P&L, blocked and qualified opportunities, completed replacements, orphan exits, settlement delays, and missing data in both cost cases.

A positive research result requires a complete evaluable 60-session path, at least 20 distinct completed replacements at 15 bps (one per trigger session, both legs filled), at least $5 incremental net P&L at 15 bps, positive incremental net P&L at 30 bps, no higher daily dollar drawdown than control in either case, and zero risk breaches in either case. Below the evidence minimum or with an invalid path: INCONCLUSIVE. Otherwise, missing a pass criterion: NOT SUPPORTED. These are fixed decision thresholds for this window, not a claim of statistical significance or permission to promote. No retuning or outcome-driven retries under SR-001.
